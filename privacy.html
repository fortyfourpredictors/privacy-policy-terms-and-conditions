Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At Forty Four Predictors, we value your trust and prioritize your privacy. This policy outlines how our platform securely handles basic technical information and standard tracking data to deliver a fast, reliable, and smooth experience across our sports analytics portal.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect non-personal, anonymous technical details to optimize app performance. This includes standard device identifiers, localized cache variables, timezone settings, and basic browser configuration profiles needed to display content correctly.

2. How We Use Information

This basic technical data is processed strictly to manage video reward distributions, maintain stable offline data sync, ensure fair access to app features, and prevent unauthorized service manipulation.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Our app uses native, client-side local storage to keep your experience lightning-fast. Storing essential data right on your device reduces network loading times, enabling instant screen transitions without putting an unnecessary load on data networks.

4. Third-Party Services & Partnerships

To provide complimentary access to premium metrics, our platform partners with verified third-party mobile advertising providers. These platforms may utilize anonymous mobile advertising IDs to deliver relevant ads and properly credit your in-app rewards.

5. App Optimization & Engagement Metrics

We look at aggregate usage patterns to discover which sports features our community loves the most. This includes tracking total view counts for high-demand updates and popular features, with absolutely no tracking linked back to your real identity.

6. Data Security & Protection

We take data protection seriously. All communications between our platform and our network architecture run over secure, encrypted protocols. Local application settings are also kept isolated within your device's native app sandbox environment.

7. Data Retention Boundaries

Anonymous status markers and synchronization tokens are stored purely on a temporary basis. Essential functional parameters, such as complimentary feature unlock statuses, are programmed to expire automatically after a standard 30-day lifecycle.

8. Age Restrictions & Protection

In compliance with global privacy regulations, Forty Four Predictors does not market to or knowingly collect technical data from individuals under the age of 18. Users must meet the legal age requirements to engage with our analytics platform.

9. Your Privacy Rights & Controls

You retain complete authority over your device environment. You can manage or clear your cache and tracking cookies at any point directly within your device operating settings. Please note that resetting your cache will also safely wipe local progress and active match unlock statuses.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect minor operational or security enhancements. Continued use of our application following updates indicates your general awareness and acknowledgment of the latest security terms.
